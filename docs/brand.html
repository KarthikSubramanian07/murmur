Brand & design system

Murmur gives every agent in the repo, on every teammate's machine, a shared view of who's working and a way to message each other. This page holds the mark, the wordmark, the palette and the type that carry that idea.

Color

Three colors and one neutral. Blue and cream carry the page, butter is the one loud accent, ink is the text. Every other step is a tint or shade of these four.

Logo

Two agents, side by side, that read as an M: two agents in one repo, talking. Use the files in docs/assets/brand; never redraw or stretch them.

Mark: the logo without the name

Wordmark

Stacked lockup: the logo with the name

Inline lockup: the mark is the M

Clear space: one eye-width on every side. Minimum size: mark 24px wide, inline lockup 96px wide. On photos, use Filled on a tile. Butter on blue is 4.4:1: right for the logo and for large text, a little short of the 4.5:1 a sentence needs.

App icons

Classic is the tab icon and the touch icon. The others are there for when a second surface needs telling apart.
